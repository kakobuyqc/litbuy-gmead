02 / The shortlist method

Three moves before you buy.

Use this Litbuy shopping guide to turn a collection of links into a comparison you can actually use.

01

Choose a specific category

Begin with sneaker finds, hoodie finds or T-shirt finds. Separating jacket finds from pants and shorts makes a fashion spreadsheet easier to scan and prevents unrelated products from crowding your shortlist.

02

Read beyond the thumbnail

Open product links and record the seller listing, color options and material details. Use a size chart and garment measurements for a size comparison. Note missing information instead of filling the gaps with assumptions.

03

Compare the complete purchase

Build a price comparison that includes domestic shipping, service fees and an international shipping estimate. Add possible import duties and payment fees to your budget tracker before deciding which item offers useful value.

03 / Better information, better decisions

A product link is
the beginning.

A useful Litbuy product links collection needs context. A low listed price, a polished image or a familiar brand name cannot tell you everything about the item behind the link.

For a Litbuy finds shortlist, prioritize information you can check: the exact variant, clear measurements, recent listing details and the terms that apply to your order.

Browse CAFANS product categories ↗

  • Fit deserves its own notes

    For streetwear finds, record the chest width, body length and shoulder width when supplied. For shoe size conversion, follow the listing’s foot-length guidance. Matching size labels alone is not a reliable fit check.

  • Photographs answer specific questions

    Where available, review quality-control photos for the selected color, stitching, visible marks and included parts. Customer reviews can add context, but neither photographs nor reviews alone prove authenticity.

  • Know which terms apply

    Check the seller’s return policy and any separate shopping-agent conditions. If warehouse inspection is offered, confirm its scope and deadlines before assuming an issue can be resolved after dispatch.

  • Keep estimates clearly labeled

    Package weight, delivery destination and the available shipping route can affect landed cost. Keep a shipping estimate separate from the final charge, and avoid treating an indicative delivery window as a guarantee.

04 / Build a useful comparison

What belongs in your sheet?

These fields make a Litbuy spreadsheet guide useful for everyday product research.

Suggested shopping spreadsheet fields
Field What to record Why it helps
Product reference Original product URL, seller name and listing title. Helps you revisit the same listing and identify changed or unavailable links.
Variant & fit Selected size, color, size-chart units and relevant measurements. Keeps a visually similar item from being mistaken for the exact version you checked.
Price & currency Item price, currency, quantity and the date you checked it. Supports a consistent comparison when listing prices or exchange rates change.
Evidence & questions Available photos, review notes and details still needing confirmation. Makes uncertainty visible before you place an order.
Total-cost estimate Delivery charges, applicable fees, estimated taxes and duties. Gives your shopping budget more context than the product price alone.
Decision status Researching, shortlisted, ordered or removed; include your reason. Turns a saved product list into a manageable decision log.

Practical answers for readers researching a Litbuy spreadsheet, product links and fashion comparisons.

What is a Litbuy spreadsheet?

A Litbuy spreadsheet is a product-link list used to organize shopping research. A useful sheet records the original listing, selected variant, size measurements, price and review notes. A spreadsheet entry does not guarantee availability, authenticity or seller reliability.

Does this page provide a downloadable Litbuy spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and comparison guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, where visitors can browse product categories.

How do I check a product link before ordering?

Open the original listing and confirm the selected color, size, material, seller information, current price and return terms. Compare available photos and measurements instead of relying only on a spreadsheet title.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

For clothing, compare garment measurements with an item you already own. For shoes, check the seller’s size chart and foot-length guidance. Do not assume that identical size labels have identical measurements.

What costs should I record in my shopping spreadsheet?

Record the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping estimate, possible taxes or duties and payment or currency-conversion fees. Keep estimates separate from confirmed charges.

Is this an official Litbuy or brand website?

No. This is an independent shopping guide linking to CAFANS. It is not presented as an official website of Litbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
